# 🔴 OPSAI – AI-Powered Business Operations Agent

## 🚀 Overview

OPSAI is an AI-powered Business Operations Agent designed to help businesses manage their daily operations from one intelligent platform.

It combines **Sales, Inventory, Tasks, Team Management, and AI-powered business analysis** into a single modern dashboard.

Instead of manually checking different records, users can enter their business data and ask the AI Agent questions to get useful insights and recommendations.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

Businesses often manage their sales, inventory, employees, and daily tasks using spreadsheets or separate applications.

This can lead to:

- Poor visibility into business performance
- Inventory shortages
- Unorganized tasks
- Difficulty managing team members
- Time-consuming manual analysis
- Slow decision-making

OPSAI solves this problem by bringing business operations and AI assistance together in one platform.

---

## 🎯 Main Objective

The main objective of OPSAI is to create an intelligent business operations platform that can:

- Manage sales
- Monitor inventory
- Manage tasks
- Manage team members
- Analyze business data
- Identify operational risks
- Provide AI-powered insights
- Recommend useful actions

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Sales Management

Users can add and manage their own sales information.

Features include:

- Add sales transactions
- Product name
- Quantity sold
- Sale amount
- Transaction date
- View recent sales
- Analyze sales using AI

---

### 📦 Inventory Management

Users can manage their products and stock levels.

Features include:

- Add products
- Track available stock
- Set minimum stock levels
- Update stock
- Delete products
- Identify low-stock products
- Get AI restocking recommendations

---

### ✅ Task Management

Users can organize their business activities.

Features include:

- Create tasks
- Assign tasks
- Set priorities
- Set due dates
- Track task status
- Mark tasks as completed
- Delete tasks

---

### 👥 Team Management

Users can manage their business team.

Features include:

- Add team members
- Add roles
- Add email information
- Track team member status
- Manage team information

---

## 🤖 AI Business Operations Agent

The AI Agent is the core feature of OPSAI.

Users can ask questions about their actual business data.

### Example Questions

- Which products need restocking?
- Analyze my sales performance.
- What should I focus on today?
- Which tasks are still pending?
- Give me a summary of my business.
- What are my biggest operational risks?
- Which products are selling the most?

The AI Agent analyzes the available business information and provides useful operational insights.
